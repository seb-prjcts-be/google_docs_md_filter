Where it runs

Three places, nothing else

A synced folder in Drive puts every .md and .docx on the Docs home screen, next to your real documents. When a coding tool touches a few dozen Markdown files a day, the documents you wanted are a long scroll away. The extension hides those rows in the page with display: none; the files themselves are not touched.

  1. 01Home screen

    Grid cards and list rows on docs.google.com/document/. A date section such as Yesterday folds away when everything in it is hidden.

  2. 02Search suggestions

    The autocomplete list under the search bar at the top of the home screen.

  3. 03File > Open

    The picker dialog you open from inside a document. Only the dialog; the document itself is left alone.

  4. –Not here

    Open documents, Google Drive, Sheets, Slides, Forms, the attachment picker in Gmail and every other website. Where it runs has the exact addresses.
