# UX Profile: MD Block for Google Docs

Builds on `~/.claude/ux-profile.md`. Only the differences are listed here.

## Aesthetic reference

- **Standard:** Custom: the family look of Seb's library sites (vanilla.penplotter, p5.penplotter).
- **Notes:** `docs/style.css` and `docs/home-overview.css` are copied unchanged from vanilla.penplotter: sober black and white, Oswald titles, Inter body, Courier New code. The large hero title belongs to that family and is allowed here. The only colour is the CGA animation: the white Docs home screen with each Google colour translated to the nearest CGA colour in CIELAB (eight colours: white, black, `#555555`, `#aaaaaa`, blue `#5555ff`, and the plus in `#ff5555`, `#ffff55`, `#00aa00`), as the p5 animations turn the p5 pink into magenta. Greys paler than CGA are dotted. Change the shared CSS in the family first, then copy it over.

## Accessibility target

- **Level:** WCAG 2.1 AA, as in the default profile. The animated GIF is replaced by a still under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## Brand tone and microcopy voice

- **Voice:** `prjcts-docs-voice`. Site and README in English, with a short Dutch section in the README.
- **Extension UI:** the popup and counter button follow `_locales/` (English and Dutch). They have their own small style in `src/popup.css` and the shadow DOM of `src/content.js`; this site's family look does not apply there.
